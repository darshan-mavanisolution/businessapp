# Privacy Policy for Rotaract Clubs

**Effective Date:** October 8, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 8, 2026  

This Privacy Policy explains how **Rotaract Clubs** ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal information when you use our mobile application and related services. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy in compliance with **Google Play Developer Policies** and applicable data protection regulations.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information You Provide
- **Contact & Profile Information:** Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number provided during account registration and profile setup.
- **Profile Picture:** If you choose to upload an avatar or profile image, we collect and store the image to display on your profile.
- **Business / Club Vendor Information:** If you register as a vendor or list a business/club, we collect the organization name, category, address/city/state, description, working hours, phone number, email, website link, and promotional photos.
- **User Communications & Feedback:** Inquiries, ratings, reviews, feedback, or support messages submitted through the Help & Support screen.

### B. Location Information
- **Selected City / Region:** You can browse listings by manually selecting your city, district, or sector. We do not track continuous real-time background GPS tracking without your explicit intent and consent.

### C. App Activity & Usage Data
- **Favorites & Interactions:** Businesses and clubs you mark as favorites, search queries, and interactions with listings (e.g., tapping to call or message a contact).
- **Local Preferences:** App theme settings (Light Mode / Dark Mode) and authentication tokens stored securely on your device using local storage.

### D. Device & Technical Information
- **Device Identifiers & Diagnostics:** Device model, operating system version, app version, IP address, and network state for network stability, crash diagnosis, and API security.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process and use collected data strictly for the following purposes:
- **Authentication & Security:** Verifying your phone number via OTP and maintaining secure login sessions.
- **Core Application Services:** Displaying relevant listings, categories, contact options (phone call, WhatsApp, website, directions), and directory information.
- **Directory Management:** Verifying, publishing, and displaying business and club listings to users.
- **Customer Support:** Resolving inquiries, feedback, and technical support requests.
- **Security & Abuse Prevention:** Detecting fraudulent activities, spam, unauthorized account access, and ensuring compliance with our Terms of Service.

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosures

**We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party or data broker.**

Information is shared only under the following conditions:
- **Public Directory Listings:** Information provided for public business and club listings (name, phone, address, services, photos) is visible publicly to app users.
- **Direct User Actions:** When you choose to call, WhatsApp, share, or open a listed website via the app, you interact directly with external applications (phone dialer, WhatsApp, web browser) and standard network protocols.
- **Service Providers:** We work with trusted third-party cloud infrastructure, database hosting, SMS gateway providers (for OTP verification), and backend server hosts who process data strictly under confidentiality agreements.
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, subpoena, or government authority to protect our legal rights, property, or safety of users.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

- **Security Measures:** All communications between the mobile application and our backend servers are encrypted using standard **HTTPS / TLS** encryption protocols. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device.
- **Data Retention:** Personal data is retained as long as your account remains active or as required by law. Once an account is deleted, your data is permanently deleted from our servers.

---

## 5. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion (Google Play Mandate)

Under Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated personal data:

1. **In-App Account Deletion:**
   - Navigate to: **Profile Screen &rarr; Scroll down &rarr; Tap "Delete Account" &rarr; Confirm deletion**.
   - Your account and personal profile data will be permanently deleted immediately.
2. **Web / Email Deletion Request:**
   - If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access it, you can request full deletion of your account and data by emailing us at **romil.mavanisolution@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"* from your registered email address or phone number.
   - Upon verification, your account and personal data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the information from our servers.
